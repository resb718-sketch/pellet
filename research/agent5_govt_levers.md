## ITEM 5.1: RDI scheme
- **ANSWER:** The "conflicting" figures are different layers, not contradictions: ₹1,00,000 Cr = total corpus; ₹20,000 Cr = Budget FY2025-26 allocation; ₹2,000 Cr = sanctioned to first two fund managers; ₹500 Cr = disbursed (to TDB, Mar 2026). Your "₹3,000 Cr" figure was NOT found anywhere — unverified. Structure: ANRF Special Purpose Fund → second-level managers (AIFs, DFIs, NBFCs, TDB, BIRAC).
- **Status:** TDB approved 22 projects (total cost ₹4,744 Cr, proposed RDIF support ₹2,192 Cr); BIRAC shortlisted 8 (₹390.35 Cr support). 30–40 more managers were expected but appointment is slow; an Outlook Business headline says the deep-tech funding push "hits pause as corpus runs short" (headline only; not read).
- **INFERENCE:** the money mostly flows to later-stage, higher-TRL private projects via TDB/BIRAC, not seed-stage founders; ₹500 Cr disbursed ÷ ₹1,00,000 Cr = 0.5% of corpus (calc).

## Closing sections
**(a) Top 3 view changers**
1. The headline RDI numbers are layered; actual disbursed is ~₹500 Cr (0.5% of the headline corpus), and the flow is via TDB/BIRAC to larger TRL-high projects — a seed-stage founder should not plan on RDI as near-term money.
2. IndiaAI compute is real and used (~58% of GPUs assigned, ₹761 Cr subsidy disbursed) — this is the lever with the clearest actual disbursement.
3. REPM: big, large-capex scheme with incumbents bidding (Coal India, L&T, ReNew); not an entry point for a first-time founder except as a downstream buyer/supplier.

**(b) Evidence that would DISPROVE "government money reaches startups"**: ₹500 Cr/₹1,00,000 Cr disbursed; second-level manager appointments slow; Seed Fund startup disbursal ₹351 Cr across 2,647 startups ≈ ₹13 lakh each (calc), stale to Jan 2025.

**(c) G1–G6:** not applicable — Agent 5 covers funding levers, not a wedge.

**(d) Unverified:** all primary documents; RDI "₹3,000 Cr" figure; IndiaAI "34,000" and "₹150/hr"; startup share of compute; current Seed Fund numbers; ANRF call amounts; NQM per-vertical; PPP-MII thresholds and 358-item list currency; REPM winners; NCMM tender.
