## Closing sections
**(a) Top 3 things that changed my view**
1. The window opened and was crowded immediately: >40 manufacturers, >50 brands, price war down to ₹325–4,200/month — the "after patent expiry" wedge is already contested.
2. API is the hard part: Dr. Reddy's own batch failure shows quality/scale risk, which supports a genuine API-quality niche but also means a new entrant faces long qualification before any customer commits.
